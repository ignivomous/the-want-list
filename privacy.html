Privacy

The Want List, for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated November 20, 2026.

The short version: the app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. I can't see your lists, and nobody is tracking what you save.

What the app stores

The items you save (their names, links, pictures, prices and notes), your categories and tags, the lists you keep for other people, budgets, gifts you've logged, and your settings.

Where it's stored

On your device, and in your own iCloud account if you're signed in to iCloud. iCloud is what keeps your iPhone, iPad and Mac in step. That storage belongs to you and is run by Apple. The developer has no access to it.

Sharing a list

If you share a gift list, the people you invite can see the items on that list and can mark gifts as claimed. Their claims carry the name they go by in the app, so others on the list know a gift is taken. Sharing runs through iCloud, between your account and theirs. Items you haven't put on a shared list stay private to you.

Price checks

To read a price, the app loads the product page from the store, straight from your device, the same way a web browser would. The store sees that request as it would see any visitor, including your IP address. Nothing passes through a server of mine, and the app sends the store nothing about you or your list.

Contacts

When you share a list, the app can suggest people from your contacts as you type. It asks permission first. Contacts are read on your device only and are never uploaded.

Notifications

Price alerts, gift list alerts and occasion reminders are created on your device. No notification service outside Apple's is involved.

What isn't collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no usage tracking, no location, and no third-party code. The diagnostics screen in Settings is a log kept on your device, and it goes nowhere unless you choose to send a screenshot of it.

Removing your data

Delete an item or a list in the app and it comes off all your devices. Deleting the app removes what's stored on that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and choose The Want List.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects any of this, this page will say so before the change ships, with a new date at the top.
