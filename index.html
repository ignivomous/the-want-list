The Want List

A want list that watches prices, and gift lists you can share. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Need a hand, or found something broken? Post it on the issues page. A link to the product page and a screenshot help a lot.

Saving an item

On a product page in Safari or a store's app, tap Share and choose The Want List. If it isn't in the row of apps, scroll to the end, tap More, and turn it on. You can also tap + in the app and paste a link.

The price or picture didn't come through

A few stores refuse to answer apps, or build their pages in a way that hides the price. The item is still saved. Open it, tap Edit, and type the price in. Sharing from Safari works more often than pasting a link, because the app can read the page you're already looking at.

When alerts arrive

Prices are checked when you open the app and a few times a day in the background. iOS decides when background checks run, so an alert can come some hours after a price changes. Alerts need notifications allowed for The Want List in the Settings app.

My devices don't match

Syncing uses iCloud. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for The Want List (Settings, your name, iCloud, See All), and that the device is online. Then open the app on both and give it a minute. Settings in the app shows the sync status.

Sharing a gift list

Turn on Gift idea for the things you'd like, then open My gift ideas on the Gifting tab and tap the share button. The people you invite need the app and an Apple Account. They can claim a gift so nobody else buys it. You won't see what's claimed unless you tap the eye at the top of your list.

Claiming a gift

Open a list someone shared with you and tap Claim. Not sure yet? Hold it for three days instead. For something bigger, several people can chip in. Mark it bought once you have it.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. The details are on the privacy page.
